below are the property of their respective owners and are covered by

- `templates/export/fonts/Century Gothic*.ttf` — the Century Gothic
  typeface, © Monotype Imaging Inc. Bundled so PDF exports of the example
  style render consistently on machines where the font is not installed.
- `templates/export/logo.png` — the logo of Thomas More University of

  mark in the default export style.

The default export style imitates the Thomas More course template as an
example only. When you build your own course, replace these assets with your
institution's fonts and logo; [docs/customization.md](docs/customization.md)
explains how.
